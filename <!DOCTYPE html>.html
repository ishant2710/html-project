ABES Engineering College, Ghaziabad

B.Tech Course Curriculum

Welcome to ABES Engineering College, located in Ghaziabad. An autonomous institute affiliated with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), we offer high-quality technical education in Engineering & Technology. Our B.Tech program is designed to give students strong fundamental knowledge and practical skills.

Course Terminology (Description List)

B.Tech
Bachelor of Technology – A 4-year undergraduate degree program.
Curriculum
The academic courses and subjects taught within a specific program.
Semester System
An academic year divided into two equal terms (Odd & Even semesters).

Available B.Tech Branches

Below are the branches offered at ABES Engineering College:

  • CSE (Computer Science & Engineering)
  • AIML (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning)
  • ECE (Electronics & Communication Engineering)
  • Electrical (Electrical & Electronics Engineering)
  • Data Science (Data Science & Analytics)

Academic Roadmap by Year (Mixed List)

  1. First Year (Semester 1 & 2)
    • Engineering Mathematics
    • Engineering Physics / Chemistry
    • Programming for Problem Solving
  2. Second Year (Semester 3 & 4)
    • Data Structures & Algorithms
    • Digital Electronics
    • Discrete Mathematics
  3. Third Year (Semester 5 & 6)
    • Database Management Systems
    • Operating Systems
    • Computer Networks
  4. Fourth Year (Semester 7 & 8)
    • Major Project & Seminar
    • Industrial Internship

Steps for Admission Process (Ordered List)

  1. Appear for entrance examination (JEE Main / State Entrance Exam).
  2. Participate in counseling and choose ABES Engineering College.
  3. Verify required documents and complete fee payment.
